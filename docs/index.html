Rain in the Forest

About this video

Gentle rain falling through a misty forest, with distant thunder rolling somewhere behind the trees. Eight hours, no music, no narration, no interruptions.

The rain is heard the way you'd hear it from inside — muffled through a wall, with the low end of the storm arriving as much as a feeling as a sound. Thunder passes every minute or so and then moves on. Nothing loops abruptly, nothing jumps, nothing gets louder than the rain you started with.

Good for:

  • Falling asleep and staying asleep
  • Studying, reading, deep work
  • Blocking out traffic, neighbours, office noise
  • Tinnitus masking and quiet-room anxiety
  • Meditation, yoga, winding down

Put it on at a comfortable volume — the thunder is meant to sit above the rain, not startle you. If it's too much, the volume knob is the right control: this mix is deliberately not compressed, so turning it down keeps the rain audible.

Recorded from real field recordings (CC0 / public domain), mixed and mastered for long listening. The video is a slow drone over misty forest, with rain composited on top.
